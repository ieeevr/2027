# Register today for the IEEE Conference on Virtual Reality and 3D User Interfaces (IEEE VR 2027)

We're thrilled to invite you to IEEE VR 2027, taking place from 27 February to 3 March 2027 at the Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre (MCEC). Registration is now open – secure your spot today and take advantage of our Early Bird rates.

## Registration Inclusions

**Full Registration entitlements include:**

* Admission to all IEEE VR 2027 Pre-Conference Workshops from Saturday 27 February - Sunday 28 February
* Admission to all IEEE VR 2027 Conference sessions from Monday 1 March - Wednesday 3 March
* Admission to the Exhibition Hall
* Delegate materials
* Ticket to the Welcome Reception held on Sunday 28 February
* Ticket to the Gala Dinner held on Tuesday 2 March
* Catering including morning tea, lunch, and afternoon tea daily

**Workshop Only Registration entitlements include:**

* Admission to all IEEE VR 2027 Pre-Conference Workshops from Saturday 27 February - Sunday 28 February
* Admission to the Exhibition Hall
* Delegate materials
* Ticket to the Welcome Reception held on Sunday 28 February
* Opportunity to purchase a ticket to the Gala Dinner held on Tuesday 2 March
* Catering including morning tea, lunch, and afternoon tea from 27 - 28 February

## Student Registration Eligibility

A student is defined as a holder of a student identification card from a recognised tertiary or secondary educational institution or an international student card. Students must be currently enrolled in part- or full-time study to qualify for the student registration rate.

## Member Registration Eligibility

A member is defined as one who is a member of the [IEEE Societies](https://www.ieee.org/communities-connection/societies-councils-and-communities/societies). Proof of membership will be automatically verified during registration based on your membership number. IEEE membership must be current.

## Cancellation Policy

<details>
<summary>Cancellation Policy</summary>

**Cancellation of Registration Deadline: Tuesday 29 December 2026**

Cancellations must be notified in writing (e-mail is sufficient) to the Conference Secretariat. Cancellations made by **Tuesday 29 December 2026** will result in a full refund less $150 to cover administration costs. As an alternative to cancellation, your registration may be transferred to another person by **Saturday 13 February 2027** without incurring any cost penalty. Refunds for cancellations received after **Tuesday 29 December 2026** will only be made in exceptional circumstances. The host reserves final decision on a case-by-case basis for granting a cancellation in extenuating circumstances. It is recommended that travel insurance be secured personally upon registering.

The Conference Secretariat must be advised in writing of any alterations or transfers of registrations.

</details>

## Conference Postponement

In the event of a postponement, all registrations will automatically be transferred to the new Conference dates. All components of your registration including accommodation bookings, Social Functions also be transferred. If you are unable to attend on the new dates, you may contact the Conference Secretariat to request a transfer of your registration to a colleague at no cost, or request a cancellation and refund which will incur a $150 administration fee up until the Registration Cancellation Deadline.

## Conference Cancellation

In the event of the cancellation of the Conference, all registrations and associated components of attendance will be cancelled and amounts refunded in full.
