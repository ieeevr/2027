# Call for Proposals: "XR Gallery" — IEEE VR 2027

Sharing Realities: Immersive Experiences Across Boundaries

## Overview

Immersive technologies have the unique power to shift boundaries of people, time, and space, engaging the senses and emotions in ways that transcend traditional storytelling. By blending cutting-edge technology with historical narratives and forward-looking concepts, including artificial intelligence and creative machines, XR allows us to step into the lives of others \- those who came before us, those far away from us, or those yet to come \- deepening our understanding of the present while opening new ways of imagining the future. Whether through virtual reality recreations of far-away civilizations (in time and space), interactive exhibits, or speculative future simulations, these experiences create a continuum across people, time, and space, connecting memory, awareness, and aspiration.

XR does not just tell a story — it lets us live it, crossing boundaries that once separated self from other, here from there, and then from now. In doing so, it fosters empathy, insight, and sometimes discomfort, alongside innovation. As the world undergoes rapid digital transformation, our relationship to time, space and others is being reshaped in real time, confronting us with new questions about the futures we are building, inviting us, through XR, to cross those boundaries and imagine them differently.

## Topics and Scope

As part of IEEE VR 2027, we are delighted to invite you to the third edition of the 'XR Gallery', fostering bridges between people from the XR and Art communities through this art exhibition, titled **“Sharing Realities: Immersive Experiences Across Boundaries”**.

We aim to provoke creative thought and discussion between artists and researchers through digital experiences around our role in shaping the world and society over time. By bridging the gap between the XR research community and the art world, we hope to inspire innovative perspectives and foster a deeper understanding of the complex relationship between technology and our world. Some of the potential questions which can be explored in this respect are:

* How do we share lived experiences with others through XR?
* What does it feel like to inhabit a body, place, or moment that is not your own, and what happens to empathy when that experience is mediated by a headset rather than lived directly?
* When an immersive experience moves us to tears, awe, or grief, whose emotion are we really having \- our own, or one engineered by an XR designer?
* What would it mean to design an XR experience not from a single, authored perspective, but as a genuinely multiple, contested space where different users encounter different truths about the same past or future?
* If presence is the feeling of being there, what happens when “there” never existed?
* Can XR expose its own boundaries, making users aware of the medium's presence even as it draws them across those boundaries?
* What is the boundary between developer and storyteller, and how is this revealed to the end-user?
* How do artists resist, negotiate, or embrace the boundaries imposed by the languages and conventions of XR platforms?

The exhibition will primarily focus on interactive XR art installations, while also welcoming performances or any other digital media questioning not only the boundaries between people, time and space in our increasingly digital world, but also more technical aspects such as how XR technologies would open new ways of artistic presentation or how to incorporate real-time human interactions in XR artwork.

Accepted submissions will be showcased in the dedicated XR Gallery space during the IEEE VR conference, excluding the days of workshops (Feb 27 \- March 3, 2027, Melbourne, Australia). Additionally, discussions are underway to arrange an opportunity for the general public to access the exhibition for one of the days.

## Eligibility

* The call is open to anyone interested in creating artistic content: artists, researchers, designers, etc.
* The proposed artistic work must not have been formally exhibited elsewhere.
* We have an important inclusion criterion: we particularly want to highlight the achievements of underrepresented communities, minorities, and indigenous artists. If you think you fit these criteria and face issues that prevent you from applying, please do not hesitate to contact us at [xrgallery2027@ieeevr.org](https://gemini.google.com/app/349ec6fb4d907490).

## Submission Guidelines

In order to apply, submit the following information directly to the PCS system: [https://new.precisionconference.com/vr](https://new.precisionconference.com/vr). The abstract should include:

1. **Anonymized abstract (1000 words)** with the following required information:
   * Description of the art piece
   * How it fits into the scope of XR Gallery 2027
   * Implementation (how it will be executed, e.g., HMD or screen-based project, performance, etc.)
2. **Representative image**
3. **Anonymized video** (max 5 min, max size 150MB)
4. **The equipment and technical information required** (needed space, etc.)

Any supplementary material (press kit, portfolio, etc.) which shows your previous work is also welcome but not required\! Anonymizing this supplementary material is not necessary.

## Use of Generative AI

In accordance with [IEEE guidelines](https://open.ieee.org/author-guidelines-for-artificial-intelligence-ai-generated-text/), the use of content generated by artificial intelligence (AI) in an article (including but not limited to text, figures, images, and code) shall be disclosed in the acknowledgments section of any article submitted to an IEEE publication. The AI system used shall be identified, and specific sections of the article that use AI-generated content shall be identified and accompanied by a brief explanation regarding the level at which the AI system was used to generate the content.

## Selection Process

Each submission will be examined by a panel of external assessors from the digital arts fields and XR research communities, on the basis of the following criteria:

* Originality
* How well it fits in the scope of the XR Gallery
* Representative video and image
* Impacts of technical contribution
* Artistic presentation
* The equipment and technical information required for its realization (including light, sound, network needs)

### **Accepted Submissions**

The art installation will be showcased in the dedicated XR Gallery space for the duration of the conference, excluding the days of workshops (Feb 27 \- March 3, 2027, Melbourne, Australia).

The authors of the accepted submissions will receive support for their accommodation and monetary compensation: each accepted submission will receive one free full conference registration. Additionally, $500 USD will be awarded to select submissions (1 award per submission) on a competitive and as-needed basis (on invoice, this amount includes a contribution towards accommodation and representation costs).

* **Abstracts and supplementary material due:** 3 Nov 2026

The abstract paper and optional supplementary material are to be submitted via the online submission system: [https://new.precisionconference.com/vr](https://new.precisionconference.com/vr)

All accepted submissions must be prepared in the IEEE Computer Society VGTC format ([https://tc.computer.org/vgtc/publications/conference/](https://tc.computer.org/vgtc/publications/conference/)) and submitted as a 1-2 page PDF.
