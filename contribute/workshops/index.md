# Call for Workshops
The 34th IEEE Conference on Virtual Reality and 3D User Interfaces  
February 27 \- March 3, 2027 | Melbourne, Australia

## Important Dates
Each deadline is 23:59:59 AoE (Anywhere on Earth) \== GMT/UTC-12:00 on the stated day, no matter where the submitter is located.

* **September 28, 2026**: Workshop Proposals and CFPs due  
* **October 12, 2026**: Notification of results  
* **January 7, 2027**: Latest date for workshops to send out their paper notifications (only applies if the workshop organizers want proceedings included in the digital library)  
* **January 12, 2027**: Camera-ready submission of workshop papers for inclusion in the IEEE Digital Library (only applies if the workshop organizers want proceedings included in the digital library)  
* **February 27-28, 2027**: Workshops (held on the first two days of the conference)

## Overview
Workshops at IEEE VR are intended to bring together researchers, developers, technology providers, practitioners, and users for a lively and interactive discussion of issues through an informal exchange of ideas and information. Workshops may target a specific application area, a specific research area, or a topic of general interest. Workshops should be more than just mini-conferences of paper presentations. They may include, but are not limited to:

* A focus on creating and exchanging new ideas and opportunities to meet new people  
* A balance of formal presentations with informal discussion and ideation  
* Emphasis on attendee participation and interactive discussions  
* Thought experiments and playing with ideas  
* Curated panels  
* Position papers  
* Live application development by both speakers and attendees  
* Inclusive VR and geographical diversity  
* Breakout groups and presentation of ideas generated by the groups  
* A summary session concluding with lessons learned, insights gained, and new ideas generated from the workshop  
* A discussion of follow-up activities after the conference

Given the expected high number of workshop proposals, each accepted workshop will be offered a half-day slot at the conference. Full-day workshops cannot be accommodated. Each workshop will be assigned a typical conference room with a presentation setup.

## Submission Guidelines
Workshop submissions require a short proposal and a call for contributions, both of which should be submitted via Precision Conference Solutions (PCS) this year.

### Workshop Proposal (up to 3 pages)
The workshop proposal is an internal document for decision-making purposes only. It should start with the following points:

* Workshop title and acronym  
* The primary organizer’s name and e-mail, which will be used for further communication  
* Name, contact information, and short bios of all organizers  
* A brief description of the workshop and its goals

Furthermore, we ask proposals to address the following questions:

* Has the workshop been organized before and, if yes, why does a repetition appear sensible?  
* How many participants do you expect for your workshop?  
* How do the workshop organizers intend to deviate from the idea of the workshop being a mini-conference?  
* Do you need any additional equipment beyond a conventional presentation room for your workshop? In general, we expect every organizer to bring their own equipment, but if you need anything specific from the venue, we can at least try to make it work.  
* Which people do you intend to involve for the workshop, for example, as keynote speakers, committee members, etc.?  
* Are you planning on publishing your papers in the IEEE Digital Library? Workshops are optional to have papers that will be submitted to IEEE Digital Library. It is a powerful motivation for authors, but it will require adhering to the camera-ready deadline mentioned above. **Please note that the conference cannot offer access to the PCS system to manage your workshop’s submissions, so you need to manage your submission process on your own.**  
* Do you have constraints on which day the workshop needs to be? Please be flexible; everybody wants a Sunday slot to save a day of travel, but that's only possible for some workshops.  
* Are there any conflicts with other workshops? Are any organizers also involved in different workshops, either as organizers or presenters? In order to control overlap between workshops, we **only allow organizers to be part of two workshops**.

### Call for Contributions
The call for contributions is a public document that the organizers of accepted workshops will distribute in order to solicit contributions to their workshop. Depending on the workshop format, the call might ask for paper submissions, talk proposals, panelists, etc. It should clearly outline the scope of the workshop, the intended submission formats, and the corresponding deadlines. **If workshop organizers intend to publish their contributions in the digital library, the camera-ready deadline mentioned above has to be respected.** For all other contributions, workshop organizers are free to select their own deadlines.
