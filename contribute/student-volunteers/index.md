# Call for Student Volunteers

The 34th IEEE Conference on Virtual Reality and 3D User Interfaces  
February 27 \- March 3, 2027 | Melbourne, Australia

## Important Dates

Each deadline is 23:59:59 AoE (Anywhere on Earth) \== GMT/UTC-12:00 on the stated day, no matter where the submitter is located.

* **December 1, 2026**: Open student volunteer applications  
* **January 9, 2027**: Close student volunteer applications 
* **January 30, 2027**: Student volunteer notification of acceptance

## Overview

Dedicated and enthusiastic student volunteers (SVs) are crucial for the organisation of a successful conference. Serving as a student volunteer is also a great chance to meet and interact with scientists from the field of Virtual Reality.

Volunteering for VR 2026 is also an opportunity to gain academic service experience, an often critical component of postgraduate work, and valuable experience in future scientific event organisation.
We look forward to receiving your application for IEEE VR 2027!

## Benefits and Support

Student Volunteers will receive complementary conference registration, along with the experience of working closely with the organising committee and meeting the wider VR community. 

Note that we are unable to offer accommodation to all the SVs this year. The application form asks whether you need accommodation. If you do, tell us about your circumstances there and we will assess what support we can offer.

***Important note:***

***The free registration for student volunteers cannot be used to register a publication. If you are selected as a student volunteer and your work is accepted for presentation at IEEE VR 2027, one of your co-authors must register for the conference and pay the author registration fee.***

## Expectations

This year, IEEE VR will take place in Melbourne, Australia. You must be willing to work about **25-30 hours** during the conference. Furthermore, you must be available for SV orientation on Friday, February 26, before the conference begins. Your SV duties will include helping set up the conference environment, assisting speakers and attendees, and more. If you cannot commit to this time frame and this amount of work, please do not take a slot from another willing student.

## Expectations

All full-time undergraduate and graduate students interested in Virtual Reality or related disciplines are eligible. We will consider geographic distance from the conference as well as diversity in the research area.

The following additional considerations will influence volunteer selection:

* An accepted publication/presentation at VR 2027.
* Good communication skills in English.
* Previous experience in event organisation is desired but not required. 
* The number of volunteers from each region, institution, and research group may be balanced to ensure broad participation.
* Students from underrepresented groups are especially encouraged to apply.
