## Overview

This year, IEEE VR 2027 will host the **17th Annual 3DUI Contest**. The contest is open to researchers, students, enthusiasts, professionals, and anyone interested in advancing the state of the art in 3D User Interfaces (3DUIs). Its purpose is to encourage innovative and creative solutions to challenging problems in immersive interaction.

The theme of this year’s contest is:

***Beyond Shared Reality: 3D User Interfaces for Shared Spatial Interaction***

Immersive technologies are becoming increasingly diverse. In collaborative settings, users often participate from different devices, ranging from virtual reality headsets and mixed reality glasses to desktop computers, mobile devices, and large interactive displays. While these heterogeneous ecosystems create exciting opportunities for collaboration, they also introduce fundamental challenges arising from differences in viewpoints, interaction capabilities, input modalities, and levels of immersion.

**This year’s contest invites participants to design and implement novel 3D User Interfaces that enable shared spatial interaction across heterogeneous immersive environments.** The challenge emphasizes interaction innovation: developing new techniques that help people communicate, coordinate, manipulate shared content, maintain awareness, and collaborate effectively despite differences in devices, viewpoints, and levels of immersion.

**All submissions must demonstrate novel techniques for shared spatial interaction.**

The proposed system should enable two or more users to collaboratively manipulate, create, annotate, inspect, organize, or coordinate around shared spatial content. Participants may use heterogeneous immersive technologies (e.g., VR, AR, MR, desktop, or mobile platforms), but the primary contribution should be the design of interaction techniques that support effective shared spatial interaction.

Submissions may address any collaborative application domain, including *education, design, healthcare, manufacturing, digital twins, scientific visualization, cultural heritage, entertainment, or creative work*, but the **primary contribution should be a novel 3D User Interface or interaction technique**. Solutions will be evaluated primarily on their innovation in interaction design, usability, and their ability to support effective collaboration in heterogeneous immersive environments.

Participants are encouraged to explore topics such as cross-device interaction, asymmetric collaboration, shared spatial workspaces, adaptive interfaces, remote collaboration, transitions between realities, multimodal interaction, or other approaches that advance collaborative immersive experiences.

## Eligibility

The 3DUI Contest is open to anyone interested in 3D User Interfaces (3DUIs), including researchers, students, hobbyists, professionals, and independent developers. Requirements for selection and presentation are specified below.

## Rules

Submitted solutions should fulfill the following requirements to be considered for acceptance and presentation at IEEE VR 2027.

**Submissions should present a collaborative 3DUI solution that:**

* addresses the contest theme ***“Beyond Shared Reality: 3D User Interfaces for Shared Spatial Interaction”***
* demonstrates **one or more novel 3D User Interface techniques** that support shared spatial interaction across heterogeneous immersive environments
* clearly identifies which interaction techniques have been created, adapted, or extended
* is accompanied by a **3 - 5 minute demonstration video**
* includes a standalone executable or clear deployment instructions for evaluation
* is compatible with the **OpenXR** standard ([https://www.khronos.org/openxr/](https://www.khronos.org/openxr/)) where applicable

**The developed 3DUI should:**

* provide a clear technical contribution to 3D interaction
* support effective collaboration across heterogeneous devices, realities, or levels of immersion
* demonstrate innovation in interaction design
* provide high usability and a compelling user experience
* be technically robust and suitable for live demonstration

**The primary contribution should be the design of a novel 3D User Interface.** While submissions may target any collaborative application domain, evaluation will focus primarily on interaction innovation rather than the application itself.

### Teams

Teams of up to **ten people** may submit solutions.

### Paper

Teams must submit a **two-page extended abstract** describing their solution.

The paper should include:

* the collaborative problem being addressed
* an overview of the proposed system
* the novel 3DUI techniques introduced
* implementation details
* a brief discussion of related work and the system’s novelty
* the design process and major iterations
* optional evaluation results, if available

Accepted papers will appear in the IEEE VR Conference Proceedings as two-page extended abstracts and must follow the IEEE Computer Society VGTC formatting guidelines.

### Video

Teams will also submit a video that presents the solution to the public, including an explanation of the equipment, software, and interaction techniques used to solve the problem. If the submission is accepted, the video will be made publicly available. Videos should have a length of **between 3 and 5 minutes** and should include the title along with the names and affiliations of the contestants. The submitted file should be a high-quality compressed video with a size of no more than 300 MB and submitted directly via the submission system or via a link to an unlisted video on an online platform like *YouTube*.

### On-Site Demonstration

If the submission is accepted, authors will participate in a demo session at the conference, where they will showcase their work to the IEEE VR community during the **IEEE VR 2027 3DUI Contest demonstration session**. The winner of the contest will be determined at IEEE VR, for which various judges from the community will try out and rate the presented 3DUIs.

Evaluation will consider:

* Innovation of the 3D User Interface
* Support for Shared Spatial Interaction
* Usability and User Experience
* Technical Quality and Robustness
* Overall Impact and Creativity

The final score will combine the expert judges’ evaluations with audience voting.

At least **one author of each accepted submission must register for IEEE VR 2027 and present the system on-site.**
